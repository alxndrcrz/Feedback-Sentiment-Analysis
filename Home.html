The AI Magic

Our Natural Language Processing engine goes beyond simple keyword scanning. It understands emotional weight, context, and intensity — the same way a thoughtful person reads between the lines.

Instant analysis

Results appear in under a second. No waiting, no batching — feedback is understood the moment it's submitted.

Your Privacy, Our Priority

Your identity is yours to share — or keep.

We built FeedbackSense for the employees who have the most to say but the most to lose by saying it. That's why we've made anonymity a first-class feature — not an afterthought.

Anonymous toggle

When enabled, your name is permanently stripped from the admin view. They see the message — never the messenger.

Encrypted storage via Supabase

All feedback is stored with row-level security. Only your organization's admin can access data from your org — no one else.

Zero re-identification

Anonymous entries don't store any user ID in the database. Once submitted, the link is gone forever — technically unrecoverable.
